About GLEAMORY

A lighting house built on one material.

GLEAMORY Ltd is a UK-registered trading company dedicated to alabaster lighting. We work directly with Spanish quarries and a dedicated craft workshop, turning raw alabaster into pendants, sconces and lamps for premium interiors.

Behind GLEAMORY stands more than sixteen years of lighting export experience — the same team, factories and quality system that have served European brands since 2010, now focused on a single, honest material.

From Quarry to Ceiling

One material, three honest steps. Every lamp can be traced back to a single block of stone.

01 — SOURCE

Spanish Alabaster

Blocks are selected in Spanish quarries for translucency and vein character. We grade every block before it leaves the ground — only stone that glows evenly becomes a GLEAMORY fixture.

02 — SHAPE

Hand Carving

Slices are cut, turned and carved by hand in our partner workshop. Wall thickness is tuned by feel: thick enough for strength, thin enough for the warm diffusion alabaster is famous for.

03 — LIGHT

Warm Electronics

Dimmable LED modules, certified drivers and fabric cords are fitted and aged-in for 48 hours before packing. Every fixture ships with a matching spare stone patch for future service.
